About AICA

AICA assesses how people use AI in their work and helps them learn to use it better. It was developed by Sasin School of Management and Chula AI Center, Chulalongkorn University.

AICA starts from people, not from the technology. It looks at three levels: people, leaders and the organization.

For you For your organization

For you

You answer questions about short work situations. Then you see what you do well and what to practice.

  1. 1. Tell us about your work

    Your role, your field and how you use AI now.

  2. 2. Answer the questions

    Each question is a short situation from work like yours. You choose what you would do.

  3. 3. See your results

    Your strengths and the areas to practice, in five competency areas.

A question looks like this*

You use AI to draft a reply to a customer complaint. The draft sounds polite and complete. What do you do before sending it?

  • Send it, since it sounds polite and complete
  • Ask the AI whether the reply is accurate
  • Check the facts and promises in it against the customer’s record
  • Rewrite the whole reply yourself


*This is not the real test question.

Five competency areas

Results are shown in these five areas. The first area is about knowing AI. The other four are about using it.

C1 Knowing AI Understanding AI Knowing what AI can do, how it gives answers and where it makes mistakes.
C2 Using AI Problem solving with AI Choosing when and how to use AI on a problem, and checking the results.
C3 Using AI Keeping people in charge Deciding what AI should do and keeping responsibility for decisions.
C4 Using AI Ethical and safe use Managing risks to privacy, fairness and other people.
C5 Using AI Continuous learning Trying new AI tools carefully and learning as they change.

For your organization

AICA can be adapted to your field or organization. The competencies stay the same. The questions are rewritten for your roles and your work.

How we work with you

Five steps

  1. Understand the workYou tell us about your roles, tasks and the AI tools people use.
  2. Choose what mattersTogether we agree which areas matter for each role.
  3. Write the situationsThe AICA team writes questions set in your own work.
  4. Review with expertsPeople from your field check that the situations are real.
  5. Assess and reportYour people and leaders take the test. You get results by area.
